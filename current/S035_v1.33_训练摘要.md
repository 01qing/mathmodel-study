## Main learning
Q1 keeps a strong no-label Frangi/Otsu/morphology mainline. Its attempted RF/SVM/XGBoost branch mostly fails transfer, and the paper explicitly identifies a repeated/overlapping source sample as the likely reason one prediction looks unusually good. This becomes a strong duplicate/source-content leakage gate.

Q2 is structurally stronger than several earlier 2025-C routes because reported periods are fixed at **94.25 mm**, consistent with `pi*30=94.24778 mm`. However some fitted traces have errors near 1 and high missingness, so the transferable route is fixed-period robust fit **plus reject**, not “every returned fit is valid”.

Q3 contributes sampling-density/equal-arc sensitivity evidence, but dense/high-resolution JRC is a numerical reference, not physical truth without external ground truth.

Q4 provides a useful Monte-Carlo sensitivity idea but overstates semantics. The sample mean of a normal angle cannot itself be an unbiased connectivity probability estimator. Mapping the sampled angles/distances to `[0,1]` using their own 95% intervals yields a heuristic relative score, not calibrated probability; interpolated dispersion is not a posterior absent likelihood/update evidence.
