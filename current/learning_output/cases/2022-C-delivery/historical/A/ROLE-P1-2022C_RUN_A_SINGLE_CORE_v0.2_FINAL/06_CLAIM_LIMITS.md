# ROLE-P1-2022C-v0.2 — Explicit Claim Limits

Status: `FROZEN`

The final bundle supports only the following claims:

- the required sealed Core wrapper identity was verified;
- all five official source identities were verified;
- four specific frozen heuristic schedules were generated and preserved;
- their event streams satisfy the audited PBS constraints with 0 detected hard failures;
- their headline scores reproduce independently from the frozen output sequences and logs;
- static canonical per-second matrices, action logs, and output sequences are frozen for provenance.

The bundle **does not support** these stronger claims:

- no claim that any schedule is globally optimal, locally optimal, or best possible;
- no claim that B3 is superior to every candidate family beyond the finite Pass-1 search actually performed;
- no Run-A-vs-Run-B superiority claim;
- no claim that role specialization improves capability;
- no claim that `9C+72` is a valid universal lower bound under the implemented asynchronous six-lane dynamics;
- no claim that the uncapped time score is definitely the organizers' intended interpretation;
- no claim that the original dynamic-formula XLSX files are portable across spreadsheet engines;
- no claim that the original candidate-search trajectory is exactly rerunnable from a preserved optimizer source file;
- no claim that same-second integer sampling uniquely determines parking-region occupancy at event boundaries.

For `result12` and `result21`, the canonical registry reports the literal printed scoring formula and separately reports a capped-at-100 sensitivity. The sensitivity is not substituted for the official literal result.
