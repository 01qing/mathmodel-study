# Paper-vs-Benchmark Comparison Protocol

## Goal

Compare our independently declared modeling route with excellent-paper routes question by question.

Do not compare papers only by final award or algorithm sophistication.

## Evidence sources

Our side:
- problem facts
- data audit
- model benchmark
- final fit
- blind/reference baseline status

Paper side:
- paper screening profiles
- full-text evidence
- reproduction status
- award verification status

## Q1 comparison

Compare:
- feature-selection philosophy
- leakage safety
- nonlinear evidence
- redundancy handling
- stability
- interpretability

## Q2 comparison

Compare:
- model candidate set
- validation protocol
- hyperparameter selection
- primary metrics
- prediction stability
- complexity/time risk

## Q3 comparison

Compare:
- whether five labels share one model or compete independently
- imbalance handling
- resampling leakage risk
- evaluation metrics
- probability calibration

## Q4 comparison

Compare:
- favorable-label semantics
- treatment of CYP3A4 ambiguity
- whether descriptors are treated as freely independent variables
- feasibility/manifold constraints
- optimization objective construction
- optimizer role vs modeling role
- interpretability of reported ranges

## Required verdicts

For every question:
1. what paper A does better
2. what paper B does better
3. what our route does better
4. what our route does worse
5. which idea should be adopted
6. which idea should not be copied blindly
7. confidence/evidence status

## Final learning verdict

Must answer:
- Which paper is more worth learning for this question?
- Which paper is more competition-efficient?
- Which paper is more scientifically cautious?
- Which elements should be combined?
- What remains unresolved without reproduction?
